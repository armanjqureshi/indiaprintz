Print methods that suit your design

The print method decides how your design looks, how it feels and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a position is one print area such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position and is the most economical option on runs of about 50 pieces and up. It suits bold logos with 1 to 3 flat colours and looks best on cotton and blends. Society festival tees and large promoter orders often go this way.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is lovely for small quantities and for a different name on every tee. Our page on DTF printing explains why it has become a favourite for startup merch and college batches.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives a stitched, premium finish. It is the natural pick for Mindspace polos, corporate shirts and caps. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so clean logos work best, and we will tell you honestly if yours needs a small tweak.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position and dyes the design into polyester, so it never cracks or peels. It is ideal for all-over prints, jerseys and sports kits on white or light polyester. It does not work on cotton.

Fabrics for sea breeze, crowds and cold offices

A Malad day can include a sticky walk from the station, an icy office floor and a breezy evening near Marve. The right fabric keeps your group comfortable through all of it.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, a safe choice for office tees, polos and festival wear.
  • Polycot blends cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less and keeps its shape.
  • Polyester and matty dry quickly, which helps promoters on busy weekends and teams at outdoor events.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels lovely in hoodies and casual tees.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smoother finish that suits merch people will wear on weekends too.

Weight matters as well, because it changes the drape and warmth of a tee. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our t-shirt GSM guide explains the numbers in plain words.
