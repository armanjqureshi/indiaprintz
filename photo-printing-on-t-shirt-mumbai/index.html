How photo printing on t-shirts works

Photos are full of detail. There are soft skin tones, blue skies fading into white, shadows under a birthday cake and the tiny shine in a dog's eyes. To capture all of that on fabric, we use DTF printing for photo work.

DTF stands for direct to film. Your photo is printed in full colour onto a special film, then transferred onto the t-shirt with heat and pressure. Because it is printed digitally, it handles photos and gradients beautifully, and there is no setup. That is exactly why we can print as few as 2 custom photo tees without the cost going up.

DTF also works on most fabrics, so your photo can go on cotton, polyester, polycot or bio-wash tees, as well as hoodies and tote bags. It starts from ₹35 per print position, and a print position is one print area, such as the front, the full back or a sleeve. If you want to know more about the method itself, our page on DTF printing explains it in simple terms.

Why other methods are not ideal for photos

Screen printing is lovely for bold logos in 1 to 3 flat colours, but a photo has thousands of shades, so it is not the right fit. Embroidery gives a premium stitched look, yet photos and very fine detail simply cannot be embroidered. Sublimation can print photos, but only on white or light polyester, and it does not work on cotton. For most photo gifts, DTF is the happy answer.

How to choose a photo that prints beautifully

The photo you choose makes the biggest difference to the final tee. A little care here saves a lot of disappointment later, so here is what we look for.

  • Sharp focus. Zoom in on the faces. If eyes and edges look soft or blurry on your phone, they will look softer on fabric.
  • Good light. Daylight photos with faces clearly lit print much better than dim party photos or pictures taken against a bright window.
  • High resolution. The bigger the original file, the more detail we can print. Photos from a recent phone camera are usually great.
  • Send the original file. Share the photo on WhatsApp as a document, not as a screenshot or a forwarded image, so it keeps its full quality.
  • Simple backgrounds help. A clean background keeps the attention on the people or the pet. We can also crop tightly around the main subject.
  • Old prints are fine. For a treasured old photo, scan it at a good resolution or photograph it flat in daylight without glare.

If you are not sure about a photo, send it anyway. We will tell you honestly how it is likely to print and, where it helps, suggest a different crop or picture.

Print only photos you own or have permission to use

Please send photos that you took yourself, photos of your own family and friends, or pictures you have clear permission to use. We cannot print celebrity photos, film stills, cricket match images or pictures taken from the internet that belong to someone else. Personal photos make far more meaningful gifts anyway, and they keep everyone on the right side of the rules.

White or coloured tees: how your photo looks on each

The colour of the tee changes the mood of a photo, so it is worth a moment's thought.

White and light colours

On white, off-white and light grey tees, photos look the most natural. Skin tones stay true, skies stay blue and bright colours really sing. If your photo has a light background or soft pastel tones, a white tee is the safest and loveliest choice.

Black, navy and darker colours

Photos can also look striking on black, navy and other dark colours. Full colour DTF prints sit well on dark fabric, and a photo with a strong subject and good contrast stands out boldly. A neat border or a cut-out around the people can look very stylish on a dark tee.

The mockup is where this choice becomes easy. We can show you the same photo on two tee colours, so you can compare before you decide.

Placement and size of your photo

Where the photo sits on the tee changes how it feels to wear.

  • Front centre is the classic spot for a gift tee and the first thing people notice.
  • Full back gives the photo plenty of room and works well for group photos with a name or date on the front.
  • Left chest suits a small, subtle photo, for example a tiny portrait of a pet.
  • Sleeve is perfect for a little extra, such as a date, initials or a small picture.

As a rule, group photos need more space so that every face is clearly visible, while a single face or a pet close-up still looks great a little smaller. Remember that each area counts as a separate print position, so a photo on the front and a name on the back is two positions. We size and place the photo in your mockup, so you can ask for it bigger, smaller or higher until it feels right.

Caring for your photo t-shirt so the print lasts

A photo tee is a keepsake, so a little care goes a long way. These simple habits help the print stay bright wash after wash.

  1. Turn the tee inside out before washing.
  2. Wash in cold or lukewarm water on a gentle cycle, or by hand.
  3. Skip bleach and strong stain removers on the printed area.
  4. Dry it in the shade rather than harsh direct sun.
  5. Iron inside out, and never iron directly on the photo.

Avoid wringing the print hard or leaving the tee soaking for hours. Treat it kindly and your photo will keep making people smile for a long time.

Can I print just one photo t-shirt?

The minimum for a custom printed photo t-shirt is 2 pieces. Many people order a matching pair, one to gift and one to keep, or a couple set for an anniversary. Round neck tees, hoodies and tote bags start at 5 pieces.

How much does photo printing on a t-shirt cost in Mumbai?

Custom printed t-shirts start at ₹200 per piece and DTF photo printing starts at ₹35 per position. For example, 2 tees with a photo on the front start at ₹470. Your final price is confirmed on WhatsApp once we see your photo and sizes.

Will the photo fade after washing?

DTF photo prints hold up well with regular wear when they are cared for properly. Wash the tee inside out in cold or lukewarm water, avoid bleach, dry it in the shade and never iron directly on the print. These habits keep the colours bright for a long time.

What photo quality do you need for a photo t-shirt?

A sharp, well lit, high resolution photo gives the best result. Send the original file on WhatsApp as a document rather than a screenshot, so it keeps its full quality. If you are unsure, send it anyway and we will tell you honestly how it will print.

Can you print an old family photo on a t-shirt?

Yes. Scan the old print at a good resolution or photograph it flat in daylight without glare, then send it to us. We will check the quality and show you a mockup before printing.

Can I add names or dates to a photo t-shirt?

Of course. You can add names, dates or a short message next to the photo or on the back. Text on the back counts as a second print position.

Can you print a photo on a hoodie or tote bag?

Can you print celebrity or movie photos on a t-shirt?
