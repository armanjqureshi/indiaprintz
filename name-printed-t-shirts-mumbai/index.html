Name Printed T-Shirts in Mumbai for Teams, Batches and Families

Name printed t-shirts in Mumbai for teams, batches and offices. Jerseys with name and number, custom name hoodies and family tees, packed by size per person.

That little moment is why we love making name printed t-shirts in Mumbai. A team tee is nice, but a tee with your name across the back feels like it was made for you, because it was.

A t-shirt with name printing looks simple from the outside, but a few small choices decide whether it looks sharp or slightly off. Where the name sits, which print method you pick, how big the letters are and how cleanly the name list is shared all play a part.

This guide walks you through each of those choices. You will see where names look best, which method suits jerseys, office tees, hoodies and family sets, how to send us a tidy name list, and exactly how pricing works, with worked examples you can adapt for your own order.

Where names go on a name printed t-shirt

Before anything else, decide where the name will live. Each spot has its own feel, and the right one depends on who is wearing the tee and where they will wear it.

Across the back

The back is the classic spot, and the one that gives you that jersey feeling. A name across the upper back, just below the collar, reads clearly from a distance, which is why sports teams and event crews love it. On jerseys, the name usually sits above a large number.

On the sleeve

A name on the sleeve is quieter and a little more stylish. It works beautifully on batch hoodies, varsity jackets and office tees where the front already carries a logo or design. Your friends still know whose hoodie it is, and the look stays clean.

On the chest

A small name on the left chest feels neat and professional, almost like a name badge you never have to pin on. It suits staff polos, hospital and clinic teams, front desk staff and volunteers who meet a lot of new people in a day.

You can combine positions too. A logo on the front with a name on the back is the most popular setup we make, and it photographs wonderfully when the whole group turns around together.

Jerseys with name and number for your team

Nothing makes a team feel official quite like a jersey with name and number. Whether it is a box cricket league, a corporate football tournament or a college sports week, the moment players pull on their own numbered kit, the game feels more serious and a lot more fun.

For most jerseys, we recommend sublimation printing. The design is dyed right into polyester fabric, so the name, number and any pattern become part of the cloth. Nothing cracks, peels or feels heavy, even after many matches and many washes. You can learn more on our sublimation printing page.

A few things to know about sublimation:

  • It works only on polyester, so it suits sports jerseys made from polyester fabric.
  • It needs white or light fabric, so your colours come from the print itself.
  • It allows all-over designs, which means stripes, gradients and patterns across the whole jersey.
  • It does not work on cotton, so cotton jerseys get names through DTF printing instead.

Jerseys start at ₹200 per piece with a minimum of 5, so even a small five-a-side team can have a proper kit. For collar styles, shorts to match and design ideas, our guide to custom jersey printing has plenty of inspiration.

Employee name t-shirts for teams, batches and offices

When every piece needs a different name, the print method matters a lot. Screen printing is lovely for bulk orders with one shared design, but it needs a separate setup for every design, so it does not suit forty different names. That is where DTF printing shines.

DTF printing needs no setup per name. Each name is printed in full colour and pressed onto the garment, so "Priya" and "Siddharth" cost exactly the same and take no extra fuss. It works on most fabrics, including cotton, which makes it perfect for everyday round neck tees and polos. Read more about it on our DTF printing page.

Here are the orders we see most often:

  • Employee name t-shirts for an onboarding week, a town hall or an offsite, with the company logo on the front and each person's name on the back.
  • Batch tees for a college farewell or a coaching class, with the batch year on the front and names on the back.
  • Crew tees for a wedding, a production shoot or an event team, with a role or nickname printed instead of a full name.
  • Volunteer tees for a marathon, a society event or a Ganpati mandal, so everyone can call each other by name in the crowd.

Employee name t-shirts also make a thoughtful welcome gift. A new joiner who finds a tee with their own name on their first day feels part of the team before the first meeting is over.

Custom name hoodies and varsity jackets

Hoodies and jackets are the pieces people keep for years, so a name on them makes them even more special. A custom name hoodie for your college batch or your office team will still be worn on chilly train rides and in cold AC rooms long after the event is over.

You have two good options here.

Embroidery for a premium finish

Embroidery stitches the name into the fabric, giving it a raised, textured look that feels premium. It is lovely on varsity jackets, zipper hoodies and polos, especially for a name on the chest or sleeve. Keep the font simple and bold, because very fine text cannot be embroidered cleanly. Our embroidery page shows what works best.

DTF for colour and detail

If your names come with a design element, a gradient or several colours, DTF printing is the better choice. It is also slightly lighter on the budget and suits bigger batch orders where every sleeve has a different name.

Pullover and zipper hoodies start at ₹350 per piece, and varsity jackets start at ₹400 with a minimum of 10. Browse styles on our custom hoodies and varsity jackets pages.

Fonts and sizes that read well

A name that looks perfect on your laptop screen can look tiny or crowded on a real garment. These simple guidelines help every name read clearly.

  • Choose bold, simple fonts. Block letters and clean sans serif fonts read best from a distance. Thin script fonts look pretty up close but can disappear from a few feet away, and they are hard to embroider.
  • Back names look good at roughly 2.5 to 3 inches tall, placed a few inches below the collar.
  • Jersey numbers on the back usually sit around 8 to 10 inches tall, with a smaller matching number on the front if you like.
  • Sleeve and chest names read well at about 1 to 1.5 inches tall.
  • Keep contrast high. White text on navy, black on yellow or red on white all stay easy to read. Avoid light grey on white or dark blue on black.
  • Use one style for everyone. Decide on all capitals or first letter capital and stick with it across the whole list.

Long names need a little care. We adjust the spacing so "Venkataraman" fits as neatly as "Om", and you will see exactly how it looks in your mockup before anything is printed.

Sending your name list and getting every piece labelled

The single biggest thing you can do for a smooth order is send a clean name list. A simple spreadsheet works perfectly, with one row per person.

If your order has no numbers, just drop that column. If different people want different products, such as some tees and some hoodies, add a product column too.

Check every spelling

Please ask each person to confirm the spelling of their own name. "Mohammed" or "Mohammad", "Shreya" or "Sreya", "Kunal" with or without a nickname, these small details matter a lot to the person wearing the tee. We print exactly what is in your list, so a quick check now saves a disappointed teammate later.

Collect sizes properly

Ask everyone to check their size against our size chart and fabric guide rather than guessing. Our chest measurements run from 36 inches for S up to 52 inches for XXL, which helps people choose with confidence.

Packed and labelled, piece by piece

When your order is ready, each named piece is packed and labelled, and the whole order is sorted by size. That means the coach, class representative or HR team can hand out forty hoodies in a few minutes instead of an hour of unfolding and refolding. Everything is couriered to your door across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai, usually 2 to 3 days after dispatch, and we dispatch across India too.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a name printed t-shirt cost in Mumbai?

A round neck t-shirt starts at ₹120 per piece, and a DTF printed name starts at ₹35 per position. A tee with a logo on the front and a name on the back starts at ₹190 per piece. Your final quote is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can every t-shirt in my order have a different name?

Yes. DTF printing and sublimation both handle a different name on every piece with no setup per name. The price stays the same whether you have one name or fifty.

What is the minimum order for t-shirts with names?

Most products, including round neck tees, jerseys and hoodies, start at a minimum of 5 pieces. Varsity jackets and corporate shirts start at 10 pieces.

Which print method is best for a jersey with name and number?

Sublimation is the best choice for polyester jerseys, since the name and number are dyed into the fabric and never crack or peel. It needs white or light polyester. For cotton jerseys, we use DTF printing instead.

Can you embroider names on hoodies and jackets?

Yes, embroidery works beautifully for names on the chest or sleeve of hoodies, varsity jackets and polos. Bold, simple fonts embroider best, since very fine text cannot be stitched cleanly.

How should I send the list of names and sizes?

A simple spreadsheet with one row per person works best, with columns for name, number and size. Ask everyone to confirm their own spelling, because we print exactly what is in the list.

Will each named t-shirt be packed separately?

Each named piece is packed and labelled, and the full order is sorted by size. This makes it quick and easy to hand everything out to your team, batch or family.

Will I see how the names look before printing?

Always. We send a mockup of your design and names on the garment so you can check placement, size and font. Production starts only after you approve it.
