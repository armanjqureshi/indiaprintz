Wedding T-Shirts in Mumbai for Haldi, Sangeet and Every Squad

Wedding t-shirts in Mumbai for haldi, sangeet, bride squad and groom squad. Custom printed tees from 2 pieces, mockup first, delivered to your door.

By indiaprints and stitches · Updated 20 October 2026 · 13 min read

It is the morning of the haldi, the courtyard smells of turmeric and marigold, and the whole family walks in wearing matching yellow wedding t-shirts with the couple's names printed across the front. Masis, cousins, the little nephew who refuses to stand still, everyone matches, and the photos look unreal. That is the moment we picture every time someone sends us a wedding design.

Wedding t-shirts have become one of the sweetest parts of an Indian wedding. They turn a crowd of relatives into one happy team, they make every group photo look planned, and they go home as a keepsake long after the mehendi has faded.

We are indiaprints and stitches, and we have been cutting, stitching and printing custom apparel at our own unit for more than 8 years. Thousands of orders later, wedding orders are still the ones that make us smile the most. You send your design on WhatsApp, we send back a mockup, and once you approve it, your tees are made, packed by size and delivered to your door in Mumbai, Thane or Navi Mumbai.

This guide will help you plan the whole thing calmly: ideas for each function, squad tees, what to print, colours that work, what it costs with worked examples, when to order and how to collect sizes from a large family without losing your mind.

Wedding t-shirts for every function

A wedding in Mumbai is rarely one event. It is a week of functions, each with its own mood, and each one can have its own tee.

Haldi t-shirts

Haldi is where matching tees really shine. Bright yellow or plain white round neck tees with a simple line like the couple's names, a cute pun or the wedding hashtag look wonderful against the marigolds. Since the haldi paste will get everywhere, cotton tees that you are happy to get messy are the smart choice.

Mehendi t-shirts

Mehendi afternoons are long, relaxed and full of music. Soft green tees for the bride's side, or a pastel shade that matches the decor, keep everyone comfortable while they wait for their designs to dry. A small print on the chest with the date is often all you need.

Sangeet t-shirts for rehearsals

Sangeet rehearsals can run for weeks, with cousins practising choreography in a building terrace or a society hall every evening. Sangeet t-shirts with the team name, like "Team Dulhan" or "Team Dulha", make rehearsals feel like a proper event and help everyone get into the spirit long before the big night.

Bachelor party and bachelorette t-shirts

The bachelor or bachelorette trip to Lonavala, Alibaug or Goa deserves its own tees. Bachelor party t-shirts usually carry a funny line on the front and each friend's nickname on the back, which makes the trip photos instantly recognisable.

Destination wedding t-shirts

Destination wedding t-shirts help a big group travel together and stay together. Matching tees at the airport, on the beach day or during a welcome lunch make the family easy to spot, and we dispatch across India, so the tees can reach you before you fly out.

Welcome kits for guests

Many families now greet guests with a welcome kit in their hotel room. A printed tee, a cap and a tote bag with the wedding hashtag make a lovely, useful set that guests actually use during the celebrations.

Bride squad and groom squad t-shirts

Squad tees are where the fun really begins. Bride squad t-shirts usually go to the bride's sisters, cousins and closest friends, and groom squad t-shirts do the same for the groom's gang. A matching set for each side sets up some friendly rivalry at the sangeet and makes for brilliant photos.

Some ideas that families love:

  • "Bride Squad" on the front with each person's name and role on the back, like "Sister of the Bride" or "Chief Dance Officer"
  • "Groom Squad" in bold letters with the groom's nickname and the wedding date
  • A special tee for the bride and groom in a different colour, so they stand out in the group
  • Kids' versions for the little ones, which are always the most photographed tees of the day

If you want a separate set just for the couple, our guide to couple t-shirts has plenty of ideas that work beautifully at a wedding.

Names, hashtag and date: printing your wedding t-shirts

The best wedding t-shirts usually carry three things: the couple's names, the wedding hashtag and the date. Together they turn a simple tee into a keepsake. Many families also add a small motif, like a pair of rings, a kalash or a little illustration of the couple.

DTF printing for full colour artwork and names

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per print position. It handles full colour artwork, illustrations, gradients and photos, and it needs no setup, so every tee can carry a different name. That makes it perfect for bride squad and groom squad tees, where each person has their own name or role on the back. You can read more on our DTF printing page, and our guide to name printed t-shirts has tips for laying out names neatly.

Screen printing for big family runs

If every tee in a large haldi order carries the same one or two colour design, screen printing from ₹15 per position is the most budget friendly option on runs of about 50 pieces and above. It looks its best on cotton and blends, which suits haldi and mehendi tees perfectly.

A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, the full front or the full back. A design on the front and names on the back counts as two positions. We will look at your artwork and suggest the method that gives you the best look for your budget.

Haldi and mehendi colours that glow in photos

Colour is the first thing anyone notices in a wedding photo, so it is worth choosing with a little care.

For haldi, yellow is the classic, cheerful pick and it blends right into the turmeric and marigold setting. White is a lovely alternative, because the haldi stains turn every tee into a one of a kind souvenir. Some families even do white for the family and yellow for the couple.

For mehendi, green is the natural favourite, since it matches the leaves and the henna mood. Darker greens hide stains well, while lighter greens look fresh in afternoon light. For the sangeet rehearsals and the bachelor trip, anything goes, from black and navy to bright pink.

Our tees come in cotton, polyester, sinker, polycot and bio-wash fabrics. Cotton is soft and breathable for long functions in the Mumbai heat, and bio-wash cotton has a smooth finish that feels lovely to wear again later. If you have a particular fabric weight in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.

Winter wedding hoodies, caps and tote bags

Winter weddings, especially destination ones in the hills or up north, call for something warmer. Matching hoodies with the couple's names on the front and the family name or hashtag on the back are cosy, practical and the kind of thing people keep wearing for years. Pullover and zipper hoodies both start from ₹350 per piece, and you can see more styles on our custom hoodies page.

For welcome kits, caps and tote bags are the perfect partners to a printed tee. A cap with an embroidered monogram is handy for a sunny beach day or an outdoor mehendi, and caps start from just ₹80. Have a look at our custom caps for styles.

A canvas tote bag with the wedding hashtag holds the whole welcome kit and then becomes the guest's everyday bag back home. Tote bags start from ₹300, and our custom tote bags page shows what else you can do with them.

When to order and how to collect sizes from relatives

Wedding season in Mumbai is busy for everyone, from caterers and decorators to printers. The easiest way to avoid last minute stress is to place your order a few weeks before the first function. We give you a dispatch date with the quote, so you can plan around the haldi or the day you fly out.

A simple planning timeline

  1. Decide the functions. Pick which events get tees, for example haldi for the whole family and squad tees only for close friends.
  2. Finalise the design. Lock the names, hashtag and date, and send the artwork on WhatsApp.
  3. Collect sizes. Share a simple list with relatives and give everyone a clear deadline.
  4. Approve the mockup. Check spellings of every name, placement and colours before anything is printed.
  5. Production and delivery. Your order is made at our own unit and couriered to your door, packed by size.

Collecting sizes without the chaos

The size list is usually the trickiest part of family wedding t-shirts. Keep it simple: one shared list with columns for name, family, size and any name to be printed on the back. A quick message on the family WhatsApp group with a link to our size chart and fabric guide saves a lot of guessing.

Do check personally with the elders, since many of them will politely say "anything is fine" and then find the tee too tight. For kids, ask parents to measure a tee that fits well today, and size up a little if the wedding is a few months away. When in doubt between two sizes, the bigger one is the safer choice for a relaxed haldi tee.

Packing by family and delivery to your door

Every order arrives packed and labelled by size, which makes sorting quick. If your size list is grouped by family, you can turn a pile of tees into neat family bundles in one evening. A cousin with a marker pen, a few zip pouches with each family's name, and the job is done before the chai gets cold.

Courier delivery across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch, and you can check the full list on our delivery areas page. For a destination wedding, we dispatch across India, so the tees can go straight to your home before you travel or to wherever the family is gathering.

Every order, big or small, goes through the same careful process that has served past clients like L&T, Siemens and Deloitte. As Google reviewer Ashwini Kodial put it: "Superb customised printing and a very prompt service!"

A quick checklist before you message us

A few details sorted early mean a faster quote and fewer back and forth messages.

  • Which functions need tees: haldi, mehendi, sangeet rehearsals, bachelor trip or welcome kits
  • Your design, the couple's names, the wedding hashtag and the date
  • Colour for each function, for example yellow or white for haldi and green for mehendi
  • Total quantity and size split, including kids' sizes
  • Any names or roles to be printed on the back for the squads
  • Print positions, such as full front and full back
  • Extras like hoodies, caps or tote bags
  • The date you need everything by, and the delivery address with a contact number

Do not worry if a few details are still open. Send what you have and we will help you fill in the rest.
