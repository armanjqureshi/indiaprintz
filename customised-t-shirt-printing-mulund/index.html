Navratri t-shirts in Mulund, and how to time your order

Navratri in Mulund is a joy to be part of. Societies set up garba in their compounds, community groups rehearse their steps for weeks, and every evening the lanes fill with music. Matching group tees have become part of the tradition, worn for practice nights, the opening evening or the dandiya competition you have been training for. If you want ideas for colours and layouts, our guide to Navratri garba t-shirts is full of them.

Popular choices for garba groups include bright round neck tees in festive colours, a group name or logo on the front, and each person's name or nickname on the back. Some groups pick a different colour for each night, which looks wonderful in photos.

A simple timeline for festival orders

Festival orders get busy for everyone, so a little planning goes a long way. Here is what we suggest:

  1. Start about a month before the first night. Collecting sizes from thirty or forty people almost always takes longer than you expect.
  2. Fix your design and colours early. Share the artwork on WhatsApp and we will reply with a quote and a dispatch date.
  3. Approve the mockup quickly. Production starts only after you say yes, so a quick approval keeps your dates comfortable.
  4. Allow for courier time. Delivery across Mulund usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch.
  5. Keep a small buffer. Having the tees in hand a few days before the first garba night means no last minute stress.

The same approach works for Ganeshotsav, society Diwali events and any celebration with a fixed date.

Print methods and fabrics

The print method decides how your design looks, how it feels and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a position is one print area, such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. A design on the front and the back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the most budget friendly choice on runs of about 50 pieces and above, and it suits bold designs in 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. A big society sports day order with a simple logo is a perfect fit.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is ideal for small quantities and for printing individual names on garba and family tees. If you cannot decide, our comparison of DTF vs screen printing explains the difference simply.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives a stitched, premium finish. It is the favourite for shop staff polos, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so clean logos work best, and we will tell you if yours needs a small adjustment.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester, so it never cracks or peels, and it is made for all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It works only on white or light polyester and does not work on cotton.

Choosing the right fabric

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, lovely for festival mornings and everyday wear.
  • Polyester dries quickly, which helps during garba nights and sports matches.
  • Polycot blends both, so it wrinkles less and keeps its shape, a smart pick for school tees.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels cosy in hoodies.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smooth finish that suits family tees people will wear again.
