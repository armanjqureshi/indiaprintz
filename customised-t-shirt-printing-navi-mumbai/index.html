Print methods and fabrics

The print method decides both the look and the cost. A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. Front and back together count as 2 positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts at ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 or more, works with 1 to 3 flat colours and suits cotton and blends. This is the usual choice for fest tees, staff tees and volunteer tees.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts at ₹35 per position. It handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it suits small quantities and individual names on the back. It works on most fabrics. If you are unsure which to pick, our guide on DTF vs screen printing compares the two.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts at ₹45 per position. It gives a stitched, premium finish on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so keep logos simple for this method.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts at ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester only, on white or light fabric, and suits all-over designs, jerseys and kits. It does not work on cotton.

Choosing a fabric

  • Cotton: soft and breathable, good for fest tees and everyday staff tees.
  • Polyester: light and quick drying, needed for sublimation and most sportswear.
  • Matty: a textured polo fabric that holds its shape, common for corporate polos.
  • Sinker: a smooth knit that takes prints well.
  • Polycot: a cotton and polyester blend that wrinkles less and lasts through frequent washing.
  • Bio-wash: cotton treated for a softer feel and less fuzzing.
  • Dry-fit: used for NSS polos where students are outdoors for long hours.

If you need a specific GSM, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our size chart lists garment measurements. Chest sizes run 36, 40, 44, 48 and 52 inches for S to XXL.

How much does t-shirt printing cost in Navi Mumbai?

A round neck t-shirt starts at ₹120 per piece before printing, and screen printing adds from ₹15 per position. So 50 tees with a front and back print start at (120 + 15 x 2) x 50 = ₹7,500. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp after we see your design.

Do you have a shop in Vashi or anywhere in Navi Mumbai?

No. We are a manufacturing unit in Dahanu and do not have a shop or office in Navi Mumbai. Orders are handled on WhatsApp and delivered by courier, usually 2 to 3 days after dispatch.

What is the minimum order for custom t-shirts?

Most products have a minimum of 5 pieces. A full colour custom printed t-shirt with one design can be ordered from 2 pieces. Corporate shirts, varsity jackets and event costumes have a minimum of 10.

Can you make varsity jackets for our college in Kharghar?

Yes. Varsity jackets start at ₹400 per piece with a minimum of 10, and most colleges choose embroidered letters on the chest. We share a mockup before production so the class can approve the colours and lettering.

Which print method is best for corporate polos in Airoli?

Embroidery is the usual choice for a logo on corporate polos because it looks premium and lasts through regular washing. For large quantities with a slogan on the back, many offices add a screen printed back. We suggest a method when you send the logo.

Can you print individual names on hoodies and jerseys?

Yes. Names on hoodies are usually done with DTF printing, which needs no setup and handles different names on each piece. On polyester jerseys, names and numbers can be sublimated along with the rest of the design.

Do you make NSS t-shirts for colleges in Navi Mumbai?

Yes. NSS polos start at ₹180 per piece in cotton, polycot or dry-fit, with a minimum of 5. Most units choose a white or light polo with the NSS logo on the chest.
