Print methods and fabrics for your design

The print method decides how your design looks and feels and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a print position is one print area such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above, and it suits bold designs in 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. Festival volunteer tees and large annual day orders usually go this way.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is ideal for small quantities and for printing a different name on every piece. Tour guide tees and cafe crew tees with illustrations often use DTF.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives a stitched, premium finish. It is the natural choice for corporate polos, formal shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so a clean logo works best, and we will tell you if yours needs a small adjustment. Our embroidery page shows what works well in thread.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester, which makes it ideal for all-over prints, jerseys and sports kits. It works only on white or light polyester and does not work on cotton.

Fabrics for sea air, humidity and cold boardrooms

South Mumbai weather moves between salty sea breeze on Marine Drive and freezing AC inside office towers, so fabric choice matters.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, great for everyday tees and office polos.
  • Polycot keeps its shape and wrinkles less, which helps for uniforms worn all day.
  • Polyester and matty dry quickly, so they suit cricket, festivals and kitchens.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels cosy in hoodies.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smooth finish that suits merch and cafe tees.

Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our size chart and fabric guide explains fabric weights and garment measurements in plain language.

For hotels, tell us if the parcel should go to the main lobby or the staff entrance, and for festivals, give us an on-site contact who will be around on delivery day.
