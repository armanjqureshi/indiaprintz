Startup merch in Powai and team t-shirts in Hiranandani

Powai has a strong startup and tech office scene, and these teams love good merch. The best startup merch Powai teams order is the kind people wear on weekends, not the kind that ends up as a duster. That means soft fabric, a clean fit and a design with a bit of personality.

Welcome kits for new joiners

A welcome kit makes the first day feel special. The most popular combination is a hoodie or a tee, a cap and a tote bag, all carrying the same logo. Hoodies are the favourite because office air conditioning in Powai can get properly chilly, and a good hoodie gets worn at the desk every day. Team t-shirts for offsites, hackathons and launches

Team t-shirts in Hiranandani offices often mark a moment: a product launch, a quarterly offsite, a 24 hour hackathon or a company anniversary. Round neck tees with the logo on the front and a theme line on the back work beautifully here. They are easy to size, they photograph well, and they cost less per piece than polos. Polos for client-facing teams

Sales, support and front desk teams often prefer a polo with a neat logo on the left chest. It looks smart with trousers, sits well in a client meeting and keeps its shape through regular washing.

College fest t-shirts and committee hoodies

Powai is home to IIT Bombay, and its well-known fests, Mood Indigo and Techfest, have made fest season part of the neighbourhood's calendar. Every big fest, at any college, runs on teams of volunteers, organisers and coordinators who need to be found quickly in a crowd. That is where college fest t-shirts and committee hoodies come in.

Volunteer and organiser tees

Bright, bold tees in a single colour per team make life easier for everyone on fest day. Many committees use a different colour for each department, such as hospitality, security and events, with the role printed large on the back. Screen printing keeps these affordable on bigger runs.

Committee hoodies with names

The committee hoodie is the one people keep for years. A front logo, the fest name and year on the back, and each person's name on the sleeve or back turns it into a proper keepsake. DTF printing handles individual names with no setup, so every hoodie can be different without any fuss.

Fest merch for sale

Some committees also sell merch to visitors. Here, small runs of a few designs in full colour work well, and a custom printed t-shirt with one design starts at just 2 pieces.

Print methods, explained simply

The print method decides how your design looks, how it feels and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a print position is one print area such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above, and it suits bold designs in 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. Volunteer tees and society event tees often go this way.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It prints full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is ideal for small quantities and for a different name on every piece. Startup merch and committee hoodies love it. If you are deciding between the two, our DTF vs screen printing comparison lays out the trade-offs.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position. It gives a stitched, premium finish for polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so a clean logo works best, and we will suggest small tweaks if needed.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester, which makes it perfect for all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It works only on white or light polyester and does not work on cotton.

Fabrics that suit Powai days

A Powai day can swing from a warm walk between towers to a freezing meeting room in minutes. Choosing the right fabric keeps everyone comfortable.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, a safe pick for everyday tees and polos.
  • Polycot blends cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less and holds its shape.
  • Polyester dries fast, which helps for gyms, sports days and fun runs.
  • Matty is a textured polyester that works well for polos.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels lovely in hoodies.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smooth finish that suits merch people wear outside work.

Fabric weight changes how a garment drapes and how warm it feels. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our t-shirt GSM guide explains the numbers in plain words.

Ordering is as easy as messaging a friend. Here is how it goes:

  5. Delivered to your door. Your order is packed and labelled by size and couriered to you.

For housing societies, mention the wing and flat, plus the name of whoever will collect at the gate. For fest orders, give us a committee member's number who will be around on the delivery day.
