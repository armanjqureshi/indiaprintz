Print methods that suit your design

Every print method is priced per print position, and a print position is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. A design on the front and the back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position and gives you the lowest cost per piece on runs of about 50 and above. It suits bold designs with 1 to 3 flat colours and looks best on cotton and blends. For 100 store tees with a simple logo, it is usually the smart choice.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, so it works beautifully for small quantities and for printing a different name on every piece. It also works on most fabrics, which makes it handy for safety jackets. If you are unsure which way to go, our comparison of DTF vs screen printing lays out the trade offs clearly.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives logos a stitched, premium finish. It is our go-to for office polos, corporate shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so a clean logo works best, and we will gently suggest a tweak if yours needs one.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester, so nothing cracks or peels, and it is ideal for all-over prints, jerseys and sports kits for college teams. It only works on white or light polyester and does not work on cotton.

Fabrics for long shifts and Mumbai humidity

A Kurla workday can be hot, crowded and long, whether you are on a shop floor, in a warehouse or walking across campus between lectures. The right fabric makes a real difference to how your team feels by evening.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, a lovely choice for everyday tees and polos.
  • Polycot mixes cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less and holds its shape, which is great for uniforms washed often.
  • Polyester and matty fabrics dry quickly and suit sports days, outdoor events and active work.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels cosy in hoodies.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smooth, soft finish that suits fest merch and keepsakes.

Fabric weight changes how a garment drapes and how warm it feels. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.
