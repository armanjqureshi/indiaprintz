School uniforms in Mira Road and Bhayandar

The stretch from Mira Road East to Bhayandar West has a large number of schools, from small primary schools to bigger institutions with several divisions in each standard. School orders usually fall into three kinds, and each comes at a different time of the year.

Uniform sets

Our school uniform set starts at ₹300 per set with a minimum of 5 sets, in cotton or polycot. Polycot holds its colour and shape better through daily washing, which matters for a shirt a child wears five days a week. The school logo is usually embroidered on the chest or pocket. Our page on school uniforms in Mumbai has more detail on the range.

PE t-shirts and house tees

PE tees are usually round neck t-shirts in house colours, such as red, blue, green and yellow, with the school name on the front and the house name on the back. Schools order these in dozens or hundreds per colour, so screen printing keeps the cost per piece low. Around 50 pieces is where screen printing becomes the cheapest method.

Annual day and sports day outfits

Annual day needs are different. A dance group may need 20 matching stage outfits, while sports day may need 200 tees in one colour with the event name and year. Stage outfits start at ₹450 with a minimum of 10, and our event costumes page explains how we work from a reference photo. Send the requirement as soon as class teachers have head counts, so there is time to approve a mockup before the event.

Society festival t-shirts for housing complexes

Mira Road and Bhayandar have many large housing complexes, some with several wings and their own committees for Ganeshotsav, Navratri, Independence Day and annual sports events. Matching tees for a festival or a society cricket tournament are among the most common orders we get from this area.

Most committees want one design on a round neck tee in a single colour, printed front and back. The front carries the society or festival name, and the back carries the year or a short line. With 100 or more tees, screen printing in one or two ink colours keeps the cost down.

The hard part of a society order is usually the sizes, not the printing. Collect sizes flat by flat in a shared sheet, check whether anyone needs a size outside S to XXL, and send us a final count for each size. We pack each size separately, so distribution at the clubhouse goes quickly.

Shop staff t-shirts, even for 5 people

The station roads in Mira Road and Bhayandar are lined with mobile shops, sweet shops, clinics, salons, hardware stores and coaching classes. Many of them want staff in matching tees or polos with the shop name, and many assume their order is too small for a manufacturer. Our minimum on round neck tees and polos is 5 pieces, so a shop with five staff can order exactly five.

For a counter or showroom, a polo with an embroidered logo on the chest looks neat and holds up to regular washing. Our polo t-shirt printing starts at ₹220 a piece, and matty fabric is a good option for shop floors because its textured weave hides creases through a long day.

For delivery staff or godown staff, a round neck tee with DTF printing works well. DTF needs no setup, so a full colour logo on 5 or 10 pieces does not carry a large extra cost. When you hire more staff later, we can repeat the order from the same design file.

Box cricket jerseys and team kits

Box cricket turfs have come up across Mira Road and Bhayandar, and turf leagues, office tournaments and weekend teams all want proper kits. Football and badminton groups order from us too. Sports jerseys start at ₹200 with a minimum of 5, and matching custom shorts start at ₹180.

Most teams choose sublimation. The design is dyed into polyester fabric, so names, numbers, sponsor logos and all-over patterns do not crack or peel, and the jersey stays light. Sublimation works only on polyester with a white or light base, so a cotton jersey needs DTF or screen printing instead.

Each player usually wants their own name and number on the back, which counts as part of the back print position. Send the list as name, number and size for each player, and we check it against the mockup before production starts. Our custom jersey printing page has more on team kits.

Print methods and fabrics

Every print area on a garment is called a print position. A front chest logo is one position, front plus back is two, and a sleeve print adds one more. The method you choose depends on quantity, the number of colours, and the fabric.

Screen printing, from ₹15 per position

Screen printing is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above. It suits designs with 1 to 3 flat colours and works best on cotton and blends. This is the usual choice for society festival tees and school PE tees.

DTF printing, from ₹35 per position

DTF handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup cost, so it suits small quantities and individual names. It works on most fabrics. Our guide on DTF vs screen printing explains when each one makes sense.

Embroidery, from ₹45 per position

Embroidery gives a stitched, premium finish for logos on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so we may suggest simplifying a detailed logo.

Sublimation, from ₹25 per position

Sublimation dyes the design into polyester and works only on white or light polyester fabric. It is the right method for all-over designs, jerseys and team kits. It does not work on cotton.

Picking the fabric

Mira Road and Bhayandar sit close to the coast, and the air stays humid for much of the year. Cotton and bio-wash cotton feel comfortable for everyday tees, while polyester dries quickly and suits sports. Polycot, a cotton and polyester blend, is a sensible choice for uniforms that get washed often. If you have a fabric weight in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Sending these details in your first message gets you an accurate quote faster:
