Choosing a print method and fabric for t-shirt printing in Bhayandar

Every print area on a garment is called a print position. A front chest logo is one position, front plus back is two, and a sleeve print adds one more. The right method depends on your quantity, the number of colours in your design, and the fabric you love.

Screen printing, from ₹15 per position

Screen printing is the most economical per piece on runs of about 50 and above. It suits designs with 1 to 3 flat colours and works best on cotton and blends. This is the classic choice for society festival tees and school PE tees.

DTF printing, from ₹35 per position

DTF handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is perfect for small quantities and individual names. It works on most fabrics. Our guide on DTF vs screen printing explains clearly when each one makes more sense.

Embroidery, from ₹45 per position

Embroidery gives a stitched, premium finish for logos on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so if your logo is very detailed we will happily suggest a simpler version that still looks great in thread.

Sublimation, from ₹25 per position

Sublimation dyes the design into polyester and works only on white or light polyester fabric. It is the go-to method for all-over designs, jerseys and team kits. It does not work on cotton.

Picking the fabric for coastal weather

Mira Road and Bhayandar sit close to the coast, and the air stays humid for much of the year. Cotton and bio-wash cotton feel soft and comfortable for everyday tees, while polyester dries quickly and suits sports. Polycot, a cotton and polyester blend, is a sensible pick for uniforms that see a lot of washing. If you have a fabric weight in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Your checklist before you send the order

Sending these details in your first message helps us give you an accurate quote quickly:

  1. Your design or logo, ideally as a PDF, AI, PNG or high resolution image.
