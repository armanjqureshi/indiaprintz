Staff uniforms in Mumbai, industry by industry

Every workplace asks something slightly different of its uniform. Here is how teams across the city usually put theirs together.

Offices and front desks

For receptions, sales floors and admin teams, the corporate shirt is the classic. Full sleeve shirts look polished in client meetings, while half sleeve shirts suit busy front desks and the warmer months. As a corporate uniform manufacturer in Mumbai, we usually suggest an embroidered logo on the left chest, which reads as professional without shouting. Our page on corporate uniform shirts goes deeper into collar and colour ideas.

Retail stores and showrooms

Shop floors are all about being easy to spot. A polo in your brand colour with the logo on the chest helps a customer find a helpful face across a busy showroom. Embroidered logos that last through daily wear

A uniform gets worn and washed far more often than an event tee, so the logo needs to be tough. Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and stitches your logo into the fabric with thread, which gives a premium finish that holds up to regular laundry. It is the usual choice for corporate shirts, polos, caps and track jackets.

There is one thing to keep in mind. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so a clean, simple version of your logo works best. If yours has tiny lettering, we will suggest a small tweak or a different method. Our embroidery page explains the details.

Other methods have their place too:

  • Screen printing from ₹15 per position suits bold 1 to 3 colour logos on runs of about 50 pieces and above, and looks best on cotton and blends.
  • DTF printing from ₹35 per position handles full colour logos, gradients and individual names, with no setup, so it is good for small batches.
  • Sublimation from ₹25 per position dyes designs into white or light polyester, and does not work on cotton.

A print position is one area of the garment, such as the left chest, the full back or a sleeve. A logo on the chest and the company name on the back counts as two positions.

Fabrics for uniforms that get washed every day

Uniforms go through the washing machine several times a week, and Mumbai's humidity is never far away. Choosing the right fabric keeps your team comfortable and keeps the uniform looking new for longer.

  • Polycot is our favourite recommendation for daily wear. It blends cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less, dries faster and keeps its shape wash after wash. Almost every uniform on our list comes in polycot.
  • Cotton is soft and breathable, lovely for school sets and office polos in air conditioned spaces.
  • Polyester dries quickly and is the standard choice for hi-vis safety jackets.
  • Dry-fit is available for NSS polos, which helps on outdoor camps and drives.

Fabric weight changes how a shirt drapes and how warm it feels. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.
