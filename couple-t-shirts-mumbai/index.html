Matching couple t-shirt ideas you will both love

The best couple tees feel personal. Here are the ideas that come up most often, and each one can be adapted to your own story.

Two halves of one design

This is the classic for a reason. One tee carries the left half of a heart, a lock or a puzzle piece, and the other carries the right half, so the design only makes sense when you stand together. It is wonderful in photos and always gets a smile.

Names and dates

Your names, initials, wedding date or the day you met make a simple, lasting design. A small line of text on the chest looks elegant, while a bold date across the back feels more playful. Our guide to name printed t-shirts has more layout ideas.

A shared photo

A favourite photo from a trip, your first selfie together or a picture of your pet turns a plain tee into a keepsake. Full colour printing handles photos well, as long as the original image is clear. See our tips on photo printing on t-shirts before you choose one.

Inside jokes

The nickname only you two use, a line from the film you watched on your first date, or the food order you always fight over. These designs mean nothing to anyone else, which is exactly what makes them so lovable.

His and hers t-shirts

His and hers t-shirts pair two related lines, like "King" and "Queen", "Mr" and "Mrs", or "Chai" and "Coffee". If your two tees carry two different artworks rather than one shared design, mention it when you message us and we will confirm the quote.

Travel tees for Goa or Lonavala

For a Goa getaway or a monsoon drive to Lonavala, matching travel tees with the trip name and dates make every holiday photo feel like a little album cover. White or light tees look fresh against the beach, and brighter colours pop against the green hills.

Pre-wedding and maternity shoot t-shirts

Pre-wedding shoot t-shirts have become a fun change from formal outfits. Photo quality and colours that look good together

A couple tee is only as good as the image and colours behind it. A few small choices here make a big difference.

Getting your photo print right

  • Use the original photo from your phone or camera, not a screenshot.
  • Send it on WhatsApp as a document rather than a photo, so it is not compressed.
  • Pick a picture that is sharp, well lit and not heavily filtered.
  • Crop out busy backgrounds if you want your faces to stand out.
  • If the photo is old or blurry, tell us, and we will let you know honestly how it is likely to print.

Colours that look great in photos

Coordinated colours often look better on camera than identical ones. Some pairs we love:

  • Navy and white, clean and easy for any outing
  • Black and grey, a smart choice for evening plans
  • Maroon and beige, warm and lovely in festive photos
  • Two soft pastels, gentle and pretty for maternity and daytime shoots
  • White and white, fresh and bright for beach trips

A dark design prints clearly on a light tee, and a light design stands out on a dark tee. If you are unsure, ask us to mock up two colour options side by side.

Sizing his and hers t-shirts for two different bodies

Couples rarely wear the same size, and that is completely fine. Each tee in your order can be in a different size, and we pack and label them so there is no mix-up.

The easiest way to choose is to take a t-shirt each of you already loves, lay it flat and measure across the chest from armpit to armpit, then double it. Compare that with our garment measurements below.

Size Chest (inches) Length (inches) Sleeve (inches)
S 36 26 8
M 40 27 8.5
L 44 28 9
XL 48 29 9.5

If one of you likes a relaxed fit, go one size up. If the tee is a surprise gift, sneak a measurement from a favourite tee in the cupboard. Our size chart and fabric guide explains fit and fabric in more detail.

Wash care so your couple tees last

  • Turn the tees inside out before washing.
  • Use a gentle cycle with cold or normal water.
  • Avoid bleach and harsh detergents.
  • Dry in the shade rather than in strong sun.
  • Never iron directly on the print, iron the reverse side instead.

Treat them kindly and your matching tees will look good for many more trips and anniversaries.

A quick checklist before you message us

Sorting a few details early means a faster quote and fewer back and forth messages.

  • Your design, photo or idea, sent as a document for the best quality
  • Matching design, two halves, or two different his and hers artworks
  • Tee colours for each of you
  • Sizes for each person, checked against a tee you own
  • Print positions, such as front only or front and back
  • Fabric preference, and GSM if you have one in mind
  • The date you need them by, especially for Valentine's Day or an anniversary
  • The full delivery address and a contact number

Do not worry if some of this is still undecided. Send what you have and we will help with the rest.
