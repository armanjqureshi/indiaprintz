Bandra Kurla Complex is home to banks, consultancies and corporate headquarters. Teams here usually want embroidered polos for staff, formal shirts with a stitched logo on the chest, and festive gift kits around Diwali or year end. The wish list is simple and strict: a clean logo, colour that matches across every size, and a garment that still looks good after a year of office laundry.

Corporate orders also come with approvals, and we are very comfortable with that. Our quote and mockup arrive on WhatsApp in a form you can forward straight to admin or procurement. Past clients include L&T, Siemens and Deloitte, so a careful sign-off chain feels perfectly normal to us.

Cafes, labels, gyms and creators in Bandra West

Hill Road, Linking Road, Pali Hill, Carter Road and the lanes of Khar are full of cafes, restaurants, gyms, independent fashion labels, creators and event organisers. Here the brief is all about personality. A cafe wants staff t-shirts that suit its mood, a gym wants kit that survives sweaty sessions, and a label or creator wants merch good enough to sell.

Colleges in the area, such as National College and St Andrew's College, add their own energy. Fest committees and volunteer teams often need a larger quantity, one bold design and a tight budget per piece, usually with an event date that will not move.

Merch people love and uniforms that last

On paper, a merch tee and a uniform tee can be the same garment. In real life they do very different jobs, and deciding which one you are making first saves a lot of back and forth later.

Merch people actually want to wear

If you run a label, a cafe with a loyal following, or a creator page, your shirt has to earn a spot in someone's wardrobe. That usually means a softer fabric like bio-wash or sinker cotton, a print placed with thought, and colours that sit happily on the fabric. A small chest mark with a larger back print, or a neat sleeve detail, often looks far more wearable than one giant logo.

Planning to sell your merch under your own brand? Custom neck labels can be discussed. Mention them when you send your design, along with any swing tag or packing needs, and we will tell you what is possible for your order.

Uniforms that survive a year of washing

Cafe staff, gym trainers and office teams wear their uniforms hard and wash them often. The priorities here are colour that holds, a logo that does not crack or peel, and a fit that works across a mixed team. Polycot and polyester blends handle frequent washing well, and embroidery outlasts most prints on polos and shirts.

A friendly tip from experience: order a few extra pieces in your most common sizes. New people join, and having spares on the shelf is much easier than reordering two shirts.

Choosing the right print method for your Bandra order

The print method shapes the look, the feel and the price of your order. Each method is charged per print position, per piece. A print position is one print area, such as front chest, full back or sleeve, so front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing for fest tees and bulk runs

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the most economical per piece on runs of about 50 and above, with 1 to 3 flat colours, and it works best on cotton and blends. College fest tees and event volunteer shirts are classic screen printing jobs.

DTF printing for small batches and detailed artwork

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is lovely for small quantities and individual names. Most small label drops and creator merch runs use DTF. Our DTF vs screen printing comparison lays out the trade-offs side by side.

Embroidery for BKC polos and shirts

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives a stitched, premium finish on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. It is the go-to choice for corporate polos in BKC. Very fine text and photographs cannot be embroidered, so a slightly simplified logo often looks sharper. Our page on embroidery in Mumbai shows what works best in thread.

Sublimation for gym and sports kits

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design right into polyester, on white or light fabric, which makes it ideal for all-over designs, jerseys and gym kits. It does not work on cotton, so keep it for your polyester pieces.

Fabrics that feel good in Mumbai humidity

Fabric matters as much as the print, especially in a city where the air feels like a warm towel for half the year. Here is how we usually guide Bandra and BKC clients.

  • Cotton: breathable and comfortable through long shifts. A great default for cafe staff t-shirts and college tees.
  • Bio-wash cotton: softer, with a cleaner surface. The favourite for merch you plan to sell or wear out.
  • Sinker: a knit with good body, nice for everyday tees and hoodies.
  • Polycot: a cotton and polyester blend that keeps its shape and colour through regular washing. Ideal for uniforms.
  • Polyester and matty: quick drying and suited to gym wear, sports jerseys and sublimation. Matty is available for polos.

If you have a particular fabric weight in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our t-shirt GSM guide explains what those numbers feel like in real life.

For sizing, our garment measurements in inches are S, M, L, XL and XXL with chest 36, 40, 44, 48 and 52, length 26, 27, 28, 29 and 30, and sleeve 8, 8.5, 9, 9.5 and 10. Share this chart with your team and collect sizes before you order, so everyone gets a shirt that fits.

Delivery to BKC, Bandra West and Khar

We deliver by courier across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai, usually 2 to 3 days after dispatch. Your dispatch date comes with your quote, so you can plan comfortably around a launch, an event or a team day.

For BKC towers, share the building name, floor and a phone number for the reception or security desk. Lobbies in large office buildings can be busy, and a reachable number at the front desk keeps your parcel moving.

A quick checklist before you send your design

Having these details ready makes your quote faster and your mockup spot on.

  • Design file in high resolution, ideally vector (AI, PDF or SVG) for embroidery and screen printing
  • Product and fabric preference
  • Garment colour and print colours
  • Print positions (front, back, sleeve) and approximate print size
  • Quantity and size split, using our size chart
  • Any names or numbers per piece
  • Neck label or packing requirements for brand merch
  • Delivery address, with building, floor and front desk phone for BKC
  • The date you need it by

Do not worry if you only have some of this. Send what you have, and we will help you fill in the rest over WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

How much does customised t-shirt printing in Bandra cost?

Round neck t-shirts start from ₹120 per piece, with printing added per position. For example, 50 round neck tees with screen printing on front and back come to (120 + 15 x 2) x 50 = ₹7,500 at starting prices. Your final quote is confirmed on WhatsApp once we see your design.

What is the minimum order for custom t-shirts in BKC?

Round neck tees and polos start at 5 pieces, and corporate shirts start at 10. A full colour custom printed t-shirt with one design can be ordered from just 2 pieces.

Can you embroider our company logo on polos for our BKC team?

Yes. Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position on top of the polo price. Very fine text and photographic logos cannot be embroidered, so we may suggest a simplified version on the mockup.

How long does delivery to Bandra or BKC take?

Courier delivery across Mumbai is usually 2 to 3 days after dispatch. We give you a dispatch date with your quote, so you can plan around your event or launch. For BKC, include the building, floor and a front desk phone number.

Can I order merch t-shirts to sell under my own brand?

Yes. We make merch for labels, cafes and creators, often on bio-wash cotton with DTF or screen printing. Custom neck labels can be discussed, so mention them when you send your design.

Which print is best for cafe staff t-shirts in Bandra?

For small teams with a detailed or multi-colour logo, DTF printing is usually the most practical. For larger runs with a simple 1 to 3 colour logo, screen printing costs less per piece. On polos, embroidery gives the longest lasting finish.

Will I see the design on the garment before printing?

Yes. After you approve the quote, we send a mockup showing your design on the garment with its placement and size. Production starts only after you approve it.
