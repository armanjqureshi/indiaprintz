Planning a large order for a school, township or company

A big order goes smoothly when the numbers are settled before production starts. Most delays we see come from size lists that change after approval, or from a second design added late. Spend a little time on the list first and the rest is simple.

Here is how we suggest planning a large order:

  1. Fix the headcount and add a small buffer of 3 to 5 percent for new joiners, new students or late registrations.
  2. Collect sizes in one sheet, broken down by class, wing, floor or department, so we can pack them the same way.
  3. Decide the print positions now, for example front chest logo and full back, because each position changes the price.
  4. Share your deadline, the date the garments must be in hand, not the date you want to place the order.
  5. Tell us if the order needs to go to two addresses, for example a school office and a sports ground, or a head office and a plant.

For very large runs we usually suggest bulk t-shirt printing with screen print, because the per piece cost drops once quantities cross about 50. We give you a dispatch date along with the quote, so you can plan your event or term around it.

Print methods and fabrics

The right print method depends on quantity, colours and fabric. A "print position" is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing for big runs

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above, and works best with 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. For most school, township and company orders from Thane, this is the method we recommend. Read more on our screen printing page.

DTF, embroidery and sublimation

DTF printing starts from ₹35 and handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it suits small quantities and individual names. Embroidery starts from ₹45 and gives a stitched, premium logo on polos, shirts, caps and jackets, though very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered. Sublimation starts from ₹25 and dyes the design into white or light polyester, which makes it right for jerseys, but it does not work on cotton.

Fabrics: polycot for daily wash

For uniforms and staff t-shirts that are washed every day, we usually suggest polycot. It holds its shape and colour better than pure cotton through repeated washing, and it dries faster. Pure cotton is softer and suits event t-shirts worn a few times. Polyester is the base for jerseys and safety jackets. If you need a specific GSM, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our size chart and fabric guide has full measurements, with chest sizes from 36 inches (S) to 52 inches (XXL).
