How to plan a large order without the last-minute scramble

A big order is mostly a list. Get the list right and the rest of the journey feels easy. The hiccups we see most often come from size lists that change after the mockup is approved, or a second design that arrives once production has started, so a little planning up front saves you real stress later.

Here is the simple order of things we suggest:

  1. Fix your headcount and add a small buffer of 3 to 5 percent for new joiners, new students or late registrations.
  2. Collect sizes in one sheet, broken down by class, wing, floor or department, so we can pack them the same way.
  3. Decide your print positions now, for example a front chest logo and a full back design, because each position changes the price.
  4. Share the date you need the garments in your hands, rather than the date you plan to place the order.
  5. Tell us if the order needs to reach two addresses, such as a school and its sports ground, or two company sites.

For very large runs we usually recommend bulk t-shirt printing with screen print, because the cost per piece drops nicely once you cross about 50 pieces. We give you a dispatch date along with your quote, so you can plan your event, term or launch around a date you can trust.

Choosing the right print method and fabric

The right print method depends on your quantity, the number of colours in your design and the fabric you choose. A "print position" is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing for big runs

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above, and it works best with 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. For most school, township and company orders from Thane, this is the method we recommend first. Our screen printing page shows how it works.

DTF, embroidery and sublimation

DTF printing starts from ₹35 and handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, which makes it ideal for small quantities and individual names. Embroidery starts from ₹45 and gives a stitched, premium logo on polos, shirts, caps and jackets, though very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered. Sublimation starts from ₹25 and dyes the design into white or light polyester, which is perfect for jerseys, but it does not work on cotton.

Fabrics that survive daily washing

For uniforms and staff t-shirts that go through the wash every day, we usually suggest polycot. It holds its shape and colour better than pure cotton and dries faster during the monsoon. Pure cotton feels softer and suits event t-shirts worn a few times, while polyester is the base for jerseys and safety jackets. If you have a particular GSM in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our size chart and fabric guide has full measurements, with chest sizes running from 36 inches (S) to 52 inches (XXL).
