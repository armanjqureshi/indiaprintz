Choosing the right print method for your design

The print method shapes how your design looks, how it feels on the fabric and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a print position is simply one print area, such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. A design on the front and the back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position and is the most budget friendly option on runs of about 50 pieces and above. It is perfect for bold designs with 1 to 3 flat colours, and it looks its best on cotton and blends. For a 100 piece town hall order with a simple logo, it is usually the smart pick.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It reproduces full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, which makes it lovely for small quantities and for printing a different name on every tee. Crew t-shirts and startup merch often go this way. If you are torn between the two, our comparison of DTF vs screen printing makes the choice easy.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and gives your logo a stitched, premium finish that lasts. It is the go-to for staff polos, corporate shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so clean, simple logos work best, and we will tell you honestly if yours needs a small tweak.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design right into polyester, so the print never cracks or peels, and it is ideal for all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It works only on white or light polyester fabric and does not work on cotton.

Fabrics that handle Mumbai humidity and cold AC

An Andheri workday is a funny mix of climates. You might be sweating on the station platform or in an auto at 9 am and shivering under the office AC by 11. The right fabric keeps your team comfortable through both.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, and the safest choice for everyday office tees and polos.
  • Polycot blends cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less and keeps its shape wash after wash.
  • Polyester and matty fabrics dry quickly, which helps on outdoor shoots, sports days and events in the heat.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels great in hoodies and casual tees.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smoother, softer finish that suits merch people will happily wear outside work.

Fabric weight matters too, since it changes how a tee drapes and how warm it feels. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. If the numbers feel confusing, our t-shirt GSM guide explains them in plain language.

What customised t-shirt printing in Andheri costs, with real examples

Pricing is refreshingly simple. Every example below uses one formula: (garment price + print price x positions) x quantity. These are starting prices only, and your final quote is confirmed on WhatsApp once we see your design, fabric and size split.

Polo t-shirts with an embroidered logo on the left chest, which is one position.

(220 + 45 x 1) x 40 = ₹10,600

100 town hall tees, printed on the front

Round neck tees with a two colour screen printed logo on the front.

(120 + 15 x 1) x 100 = ₹13,500

50 team day tees, front and back

Round neck tees, screen printed on the front with the logo and on the back with the event line.

(120 + 15 x 2) x 50 = ₹7,500

15 crew t-shirts for an Andheri West shoot

Round neck tees with a full colour DTF print on the front and each crew member's role on the back.

Seeing the maths laid out like this makes budgeting much easier, and you can play with the numbers yourself. Dropping the back print, switching from DTF to screen printing on a bigger run, or choosing round necks over polos can all bring the cost down without losing the look you want.
