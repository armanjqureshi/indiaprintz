Choosing a print method

The print method changes the look, the feel and the price. Each method is priced per print position, and a print position is one area such as front chest, full back or sleeve. Front plus back counts as two positions.

Screen printing

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position. It is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 and above, for designs with 1 to 3 flat colours. It works best on cotton and blends, which makes it a good fit for town hall and fest tees.

DTF printing

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it suits small quantities, crew tees with individual names and startup merch. If you are unsure between the two, read our comparison of DTF vs screen printing.

Embroidery

Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position. It gives a stitched, premium logo and is the usual choice for staff polos, corporate shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so simple logos work best.

Sublimation

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position. It dyes the design into polyester, works only on white or light fabric and is used for all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It does not work on cotton.

Fabric for Mumbai humidity and AC offices

Andheri staff spend part of the day in humid outdoor air, on the station platform or in an auto, and part of it in cold air-conditioned offices. The fabric needs to handle both.

  • Cotton is soft and breathable, and is the safest choice for everyday office tees and polos.
  • Polycot blends cotton and polyester, so it wrinkles less and keeps its shape after many washes.
  • Polyester and matty fabrics dry fast, which helps for outdoor shoots, sports days and events in the heat.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that works well for hoodies and casual tees.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smoother, softer finish and suits merch people will wear outside work.

Fabric weight also matters. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our t-shirt GSM guide explains what the numbers mean for comfort and feel.

Checklist before you order

A few details sorted early save a round of messages later.
