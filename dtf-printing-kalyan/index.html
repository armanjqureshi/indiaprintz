What DTF printing is and how it works

DTF stands for direct to film. Instead of printing straight onto the fabric, the design is first printed onto a special film and then transferred to the garment with heat and pressure. It sounds technical, but the steps are simple when you see them one by one.

  1. Your artwork is set to print size. We place the design at the exact width it will appear on the tee, whether that is a small chest logo or a full back print.
  2. The design is printed onto film. Every colour goes down in one pass, and a layer of white ink is printed behind the colours so they stay bright on any shade of fabric.
  3. Adhesive powder is added and cured. A fine powder is spread over the wet ink and gently heated, so it melts into a thin glue layer behind the print.
  4. The film is heat pressed onto the garment. Heat and pressure bond the print to the surface of the fabric.
  5. The film is peeled away. What stays behind is your design, sharp and flexible, sitting neatly on the tee.

Because there are no screens to prepare, there is no setup for each design or each colour. That one fact explains why DTF is the favourite for small runs, single pieces with different names, and artwork with dozens or even hundreds of colours. When to choose DTF, and when another method is better

Every print method has its sweet spot, and choosing well saves you money and gives you a better finish. Here is when DTF wins.

  • Your design has photos, gradients, shadows or lots of colours.
  • You need fewer than about 50 pieces.
  • Every piece needs a different name or number.
  • You want to print on dark fabric, or on a mix of fabrics in one order.
  • You want to try a design on a small batch before ordering more.

Now the honest side. If you are ordering 50 or more tees with a simple design in 1 to 3 flat colours, screen printing in Kalyan will usually cost less per piece. If you want a stitched, premium logo on polos, shirts or caps, embroidery in Kalyan gives a finish that print cannot match. And for all-over patterns on white or light polyester jerseys, sublimation printing in Kalyan dyes the design right into the fabric. We are always happy to suggest the right one once we see your artwork.

Artwork and file tips for DTF

DTF reproduces what you send very faithfully, which is wonderful, but it also means the quality of your file shows up on the fabric. A few small habits make a big difference.

  • Send a high resolution PNG. Aim for around 300 dpi at the size you want printed. A tiny image stretched to a full back print will look blurry.
  • Use a transparent background. If your design sits on a white or coloured box, that box will print too. Remove it unless you actually want it.
  • Keep thin lines bold enough. Hairline strokes and very small text can lose detail, so thicken them slightly if the print is small.
  • Brighten dull photos. A dark phone photo prints dark. Crop it, lift the brightness a little and send the original file, not a screenshot.
  • Share names in a sheet. For name orders, send a simple list with each name and its size next to it, and check spellings twice.
  • Tell us the placement. Front chest, full front, back, sleeve or neck, so the mockup matches your plan.

Not sure if your file is good enough? Send it anyway. We will tell you honestly and suggest a fix before anything is printed.

Fabric pairing for DTF printing

One of the nicest things about DTF is how easy going it is about fabric. It works on cotton, polyester, sinker, polycot and bio-wash, in light or dark colours. That flexibility means you can match the fabric to the occasion instead of to the print method.

Cotton round necks are the natural choice for fest and class tees, because they are soft and breathable through a long, hot day on campus. Polyester suits safety jackets and anything worn outdoors in the rain. Sinker feels lovely in hoodies, and bio-wash cotton has a smooth surface that makes photo prints look especially crisp.

A large solid block of print feels a touch heavier than the fabric around it, so designs with some open space breathe better in Kalyan's humid months. If you have a fabric weight in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.

How long DTF prints last, and how to care for them

A well pressed DTF print is flexible and stretches with the fabric, so it does not crack the way old style rubbery prints used to. With a little care, it stays bright through many washes and many fest reunions.

  • Wait at least a day before the first wash.
  • Turn the garment inside out before washing.
  • Use cold or lukewarm water and a mild detergent, with no bleach.
  • Never iron directly on the print. Iron inside out or place a cloth over it.
  • Dry in the shade and avoid very hot tumble drying.

Follow these and the tee from this year's fest will still look good when the batch meets again years later.

Frequently asked questions

How much does DTF printing cost in Kalyan?

DTF printing starts at ₹35 per print position, added to the garment price. For example, 35 round neck tees printed front and back start at ₹6,650. Your final price is confirmed on WhatsApp once we see your design and sizes.

Can I get just 2 DTF printed t-shirts in Kalyan?

Yes. Custom printed t-shirts with one full colour design start at just 2 pieces. Two tees with one front print start at ₹470, which makes DTF perfect for anniversaries, birthdays and gifts.

Can DTF print a different name on every t-shirt?

Yes, and this is one of its biggest strengths. Since DTF has no setup per design, each tee can carry its own name or number. Send us a list with each name and its size so we can match them correctly.

Does DTF printing work on black and navy t-shirts?

Yes. DTF prints a white layer behind your colours, so they stay bright on black, navy, maroon and other dark fabrics. Your mockup will show how the design looks on your chosen colour.

Is DTF better than screen printing for 30 fest tees?

For 30 tees, especially with a colourful or gradient design, DTF is usually the better pick because there is no setup per colour. If you are ordering 50 or more tees with a simple 1 to 3 colour design, screen printing usually works out cheaper per piece.

Will a DTF print crack or peel after washing?

A properly pressed DTF print is flexible and holds up well through regular washing. Wash it inside out in cold or lukewarm water, skip bleach, and never iron directly on the print to keep it looking fresh.

What file should I send for DTF printing?

A high resolution PNG with a transparent background works best, ideally around 300 dpi at print size. Vector files such as PDF or AI are welcome too. If your file needs work, we will tell you before printing.
