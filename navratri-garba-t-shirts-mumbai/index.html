Navratri T-Shirt Printing: Navratri and Garba T-Shirts in Mumbai

Custom Navratri t-shirt printing in Mumbai for garba groups, societies and event crew. From 5 pieces, mockup first, packed by size and delivered to your door.

The dhol kicks in, the circle forms, and suddenly thirty people from the same building are spinning in perfect step, every one of them in the same bright Navratri t-shirt. For nine nights, Mumbai turns into one giant dance floor. Society compounds clear out their parked cars, and the big garba grounds in Borivali, Mulund, Ghatkopar and Goregaon fill up with thousands of dancers, lights and music that carries for streets.

Look closely at any of those grounds and you will spot the groups. A cluster in matching orange here, a ring of cousins in deep green there, a dance class with their name printed across the back. Matching tees make a group easy to find in the crowd, and they make every photo from the night look like it was planned, which it was.

Garba t-shirts for dance groups and societies

Most of the garba t-shirts we make start with a small group of people who simply want to dance together and look the part. The style changes a little depending on who is ordering, so here is how the usual groups approach it.

Housing societies and building groups

Society garba is where a lot of the magic happens. Neighbours who nod at each other in the lift all year suddenly become a team for nine nights. Society tees usually carry the building or society name on the front, sometimes with a small dandiya or diya motif, and a fun line on the back. Many societies order a mix of adult sizes so every family in the wing can join in.

Dance classes and performance troupes

Garba classes and dance troupes tend to want a sharper look, because they are often performing or competing. They like the class name in large letters on the back, a logo on the front chest, and sometimes each dancer's name on the sleeve. Dandiya group t-shirts for a troupe often come in two or three coordinated colours so the formation looks striking from the stands.

Friends, families and office groups

Then there are the groups of friends who go to the same ground every year, the big joint families, and office colleagues who head out together after work. These Navratri group t-shirts in Mumbai are often playful, with inside jokes, nicknames or a family surname on the back. Small runs are completely fine. Most products start at just 5 pieces, so even a tight group of cousins can have their own design.

If you want ideas beyond festival wear, our main custom t-shirt printing guide covers styles, necklines and everyday designs.

Garba event volunteer t-shirts, crew and sponsor tees

Behind every smooth garba night is a team most dancers never notice. Volunteers manage the entry gates, guide people to the right ring, look after lost children and keep the water stations running. For these teams, a clear, readable tee is a practical tool as much as a uniform.

Volunteer and help desk tees

Garba event volunteer t-shirts work best in a bold colour that stands out under stage lights, with a large word like VOLUNTEER or HELP DESK across the back. Visitors should be able to spot a volunteer from across the ground without having to ask. Keeping the front simple, with just the event logo, helps the back message do its job.

Crew, stage and backstage teams

Sound, lighting, stage and decor crews often prefer a darker tee that does not distract on stage, with CREW or the team role printed on the back. Some events give separate colours to each team, so the stage manager can tell at a glance who handles what. Caps help a lot for crews working through the afternoon setup.

Sponsor tees

Many garba events print sponsor logos on volunteer and crew tees. A common layout is the event logo on the front, the main sponsor across the upper back and smaller partner logos on the sleeves. Since each print area is priced as one position, planning the layout early keeps the budget clear and stops logos from fighting for space.

Navratri colour t-shirts for every night

One of the most loved Navratri traditions in Mumbai is dressing in a particular colour on each of the nine days. Each day is linked to a colour, and many people, from college students to office teams, plan their outfits around it. Walk into a local train or an office on any Navratri morning and you will see the same colour popping up everywhere.

The colours change from year to year, since they follow the calendar, so we always suggest checking the list for the current year before you order. Once you have it, you can decide how many of the nine days you want your group to match.

This is why Navratri colour t-shirts are so popular with groups. Rather than one tee for the whole festival, many groups order the same design in several colours. A society might pick three or four of the year's colours for the weekend nights, while a dance troupe might choose two colours that also suit their performance. The design stays the same, only the tee colour changes, so it all still feels like one set.

A few small tips help here. Choose a print colour that reads well on every tee colour you have picked, such as white on dark tees and a deep colour on light ones. Tell us the size split for each colour separately, since people often want more of some colours than others. And keep the colour list handy when you message us, so the mockups can be checked against it.

Fabrics for hours of dancing in October heat

Garba is a proper workout. Dancers are on their feet for hours, spinning and clapping under lights, and the October air in Mumbai can still be warm and sticky. The fabric you choose makes a big difference to how your group feels at midnight.

  • Polyester handles sweat well and dries quickly, so it is the favourite for serious dancers, troupes and anyone who plans to dance until the last song. It also holds bright colours beautifully.
  • Cotton is soft, breathable and gentle on the skin. It suits society groups, families and people who want a comfortable tee they can wear again after the festival.
  • Polycot blends cotton and polyester, giving you some of the softness of cotton with less wrinkling and better shape after washing.
  • Sinker is a soft knit that feels relaxed and casual, nice for friends' group tees.
  • Bio-wash cotton has a smoother finish, which works well for designs people want to wear on regular days too.

Fabric weight also changes how a tee feels on a hot night. Lighter fabric breathes more, while heavier fabric feels sturdier. Tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability, or simply tell us how your group will use the tees and we will suggest something that fits.

Screen printing or DTF for your garba t-shirts

The print method decides how your design looks and what it costs. Each method is priced per print position, and a position is simply one print area, like the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. Front and back together count as two positions.

Screen printing for big groups

Screen printing starts from ₹15 per position and is the most economical choice on runs of about 50 pieces and above. It works best for bold designs in 1 to 3 flat colours, and it looks great on cotton and blends. A large society order or a volunteer team with a simple logo is the perfect match. Our screen printing page explains the process in more detail.

DTF for colourful artwork and names

DTF printing starts from ₹35 per position. It handles full colour artwork, photos and gradients with no setup, so it is ideal for vibrant Navratri designs with dandiya sticks, mirror work patterns or illustrated dancers. Because there is no setup, every tee can carry a different name, which dance groups and families love. Read more on our DTF printing page, or see our guide to name printed t-shirts for personalised ideas.

A note on sublimation and embroidery

Sublimation starts from ₹25 per position and dyes all-over designs into white or light polyester, which can look stunning for a troupe. It does not work on cotton. Embroidery starts from ₹45 per position and suits caps and polos for organising teams, though very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered.

Planning your Navratri t-shirts: timeline, sizes and packing

Navratri is peak season for custom apparel. Societies, dance classes, colleges, offices and event organisers all place orders in the same few weeks, so planning early is the single best thing you can do.

When to order

We recommend sending your design 2 to 3 weeks before the first night of Navratri. That leaves comfortable time for the quote, the mockup approval and production, and it gives you a buffer for last-minute additions when three more neighbours decide to join. We give you a dispatch date with the quote, so you know exactly where you stand.

Here is how an order comes together:

  1. Send your design on WhatsApp with the product, colours, quantity and rough size split.
  2. Get a quote and a dispatch date with the fabric and print method confirmed.
  3. Approve the mockup of your design on the garment, checking placement, size and colours.
  4. Production at our own unit, where cutting, stitching and printing all happen under one roof.
  5. Courier delivery to your door, packed and labelled by size.

Past clients include L&T, Siemens and Deloitte, and a 40 piece society order goes through the same careful process as a large corporate one. As Google reviewer Ashwini Kodial put it: "Superb customised printing and a very prompt service!"

Getting sizes right

Collecting sizes from a whole society can feel like herding cats. A simple shared sheet or a message in the society group, with names and sizes, works wonders. Our garment chest measurements are 36, 40, 44, 48 and 52 inches for S, M, L, XL and XXL, with lengths from 26 to 30 inches. Since dancers move a lot, many people like going one size up for a relaxed fit. Our size chart and fabric guide has the full measurements.

Packing and delivery

Every order is packed and labelled by size, and by colour when you order several colours, so distributing tees in the society clubhouse or at the event office takes minutes. Courier delivery across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch. Our delivery areas page lists the localities we cover, and we dispatch across India too.

A quick checklist before you message us

A few details sorted early mean a faster quote and fewer back and forth messages.

  • Your design or logo in the best quality you have, ideally a vector file or a high resolution PNG
  • The product and tee colours, including this year's Navratri colours if your group is matching by day
  • Quantity and size split for each colour
  • Print positions, such as front, back and sleeve
  • Any names to be printed, spelt exactly as they should appear
  • Fabric preference, polyester for heavy dancing or cotton for comfort
  • Add-ons like caps or tote bags
  • The date you need everything by and the full delivery address with a contact number

Do not worry if some of this is still open. Send what you have, and we will help you fill in the rest.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Navratri t-shirts cost in Mumbai?

Round neck t-shirts start at ₹120 per piece before printing, and screen printing starts at ₹15 per position. For example, 60 tees screen printed on the front and back start at ₹9,000. Your final price is confirmed on WhatsApp once we see your design.

What is the minimum order for garba group t-shirts?

Most products, including round neck tees, polos and caps, start at 5 pieces. Full colour custom printed t-shirts with one design start at just 2 pieces. Event costumes start at 10 pieces.

How early should I order t-shirts for Navratri?

We suggest ordering 2 to 3 weeks before Navratri begins, since it is peak season. We give a dispatch date with the quote, and courier delivery in Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch.

Which fabric is best for garba t-shirts?

Polyester is best for long hours of dancing because it handles sweat and dries quickly. Cotton is softer and more breathable for relaxed society groups. Polycot sits in between and keeps its shape well.

Can I order the same design in different Navratri colours?

Yes. Many groups order one design in several of the year's Navratri colours. Just share the size split for each colour, and we pack and label each colour by size.

Can you print names on dandiya group t-shirts?

Yes. DTF printing needs no setup, so every tee can carry a different name or nickname. It works well for dance troupes, families and friends' groups.

Do you make t-shirts for garba event volunteers and crews?

Yes. We print volunteer, crew and help desk tees with large readable text on the back, and sponsor logos on the back or sleeves. Caps with the event logo are popular for teams working during setup.

Will I see a mockup before my Navratri t-shirts are printed?

Always. We send a mockup of your design on the garment so you can check placement, size and colours. Production starts only after you approve it.
