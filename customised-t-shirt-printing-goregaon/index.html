Exhibition t-shirts for NESCO, planned backwards from the event date

NESCO in Goregaon East runs exhibitions through the year, and many exhibitors only think about uniforms once the stall design is final. By then the show can be close. The simplest way to avoid a rush is to start from the day the stall opens and work backwards.

When you message us about exhibition t-shirts for NESCO, share these details first:

  • Event name and dates
  • Hall and stall number, so cartons can be labelled correctly if you run more than one stall or send kits to different teams
  • Number of staff and their sizes
  • Items needed, for example stall staff polos, promoter tees, caps and tote bags
  • Logo file and the garment colours you want

We reply with a quote and a dispatch date. Courier delivery within Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch. Add that to the dispatch date, then keep a few days of buffer before the show so you can check sizes and hand out uniforms. If the dispatch date does not leave enough buffer, we tell you at the quote stage, so you can reduce the item list or simplify the design before anything is printed.

A few habits that help on exhibition orders:

  1. Approve the mockup quickly. Production starts only after you approve it.
  2. Use one logo file across polos, caps and totes, so a single set of artwork covers the whole kit.
  3. Keep the stall staff in one colour and the promoters in another. Visitors find the right person faster.
  4. Order a couple of extra pieces in the most common sizes for staff who are added at the last minute.

Crew t-shirts for Film City and studio shoots

Film City in Goregaon East and the studios around it keep a steady flow of shoots, and crews like having their own show merch. The usual layout is the show name on the front and the department on the back: CAMERA, LIGHTS, PRODUCTION, ART, COSTUME, DIRECTION. Everyone gets the same front, and only the back print changes.

For crew t-shirts in Film City, DTF printing is often the practical choice. It needs no setup, prints full colour, and each department can have a different back print without extra cost per design. If one department is large, say 50 or more people, screen printing that batch brings the per piece price down.

Night shoots, early call times and air-conditioned floors mean hoodies get worn as much as tees. Crews working outdoors near Aarey Colony in the cooler months tend to ask for custom hoodies with the same front and back layout as the tees. Pullover and zipper hoodies both start at ₹350 before printing.

Send the crew list as a sheet with name, department and size. We pack and label by size, which makes distribution on set much quicker.

Custom caps and tote bags for giveaways that people keep

At a busy exhibition, most flyers and freebies end up in a bin at the exit. A canvas tote is different, because people use it for groceries, books and lunch, and your logo goes with them. A cap gets worn at the next outdoor event or weekend trip.

Some practical points for custom caps and tote bags for giveaways:

  • On totes, a single colour logo on natural canvas is simple and reads clearly from a distance. Screen printing suits this well on runs of 50 or more.
  • On caps, an embroidered front logo looks more finished than a print and holds up to sweat and sun. See our custom caps page for styles.
  • Keep the logo large enough to read on a tote, and keep fine text off caps, since very fine text cannot be embroidered.
  • For welcome kits, polyester backpacks are an option alongside canvas totes. Details are on our custom tote bags page.

Caps start at ₹80 and totes or backpacks at ₹300, both with a minimum of 5.

Print methods and fabric for customised t-shirt printing in Goregaon

Each print method is priced per print position, per piece. A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing, from ₹15

Screen printing is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 or more. It suits designs with 1 to 3 flat colours and works best on cotton and blends. Promoter tees for an exhibition and event tees for an office are typical screen print jobs.

DTF printing, from ₹35

DTF prints full colour, photos and gradients with no setup. It is good for small quantities and for individual names, which makes it a natural fit for crew tees with different departments. It works on most fabrics. If you are unsure which to pick, our guide on DTF vs screen printing compares the two.

Embroidery, from ₹45

Embroidery gives a stitched, premium finish on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. It is the usual choice for stall staff polos and office uniforms. Very fine text and photographs cannot be embroidered, so logos may need slight simplification.

Sublimation, from ₹25

Sublimation dyes the design into polyester fabric, so it suits white or light polyester garments, all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It does not work on cotton.

Choosing the fabric

Cotton is comfortable and the best base for screen printing. Polyester dries fast and is needed for sublimation. Polycot blends cotton and polyester, and holds shape well for uniforms worn every day. Sinker is a soft knit used for tees and hoodies, matty is a textured fabric common on polos, and bio-wash cotton has a smoother, softer finish.

For long days on an exhibition floor under stall lights, many teams pick polyester or polycot polos. For crew tees worn on outdoor shoots, cotton or bio-wash is more comfortable. If you need a particular fabric weight, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.

How much does t-shirt printing cost in Goregaon?

Round neck t-shirts start at ₹120 per piece, and printing is added per position, from ₹15 for screen printing or ₹35 for DTF. For example, 50 tees with a screen printed front and back start at ₹7,500. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp after we see your design.

Can you deliver exhibition t-shirts before my NESCO event?

Share the event dates, hall and stall number when you first message us. We give a dispatch date with the quote, and courier delivery within Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after that. If the timing is tight, we tell you before you confirm.

What is the minimum order for custom t-shirts?
