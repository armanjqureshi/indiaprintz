Exhibition t-shirts for NESCO, planned from the show date backwards

NESCO in Goregaon East hosts exhibitions through the year, and many exhibitors only start thinking about uniforms once the stall design is locked. That is completely normal, and it is also why a little planning goes such a long way. The easiest method is to start from the day your stall opens and count backwards.

When you message us about exhibition t-shirts for NESCO, it helps to share these up front:

  • The event name and dates
  • Your hall and stall number, so cartons can be labelled clearly if kits go to different teams
  • The number of staff and each person's size
  • The items you need, such as stall staff polos, promoter tees, caps and tote bags
  • Your logo file and the garment colours you have in mind

We reply with a quote and a dispatch date. Courier delivery across Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch, so add that on and then keep a few days of breathing room before the show. That buffer gives you time to open the cartons, check sizes and hand everything out calmly. If the dispatch date leaves you too little room, we say so at the quote stage, so you can trim the item list or simplify the design before anything is printed.

Little habits that make exhibition orders easy

  1. Approve the mockup quickly, since production begins only once you say yes.
  2. Use one logo file across polos, caps and totes, so a single piece of artwork covers the whole kit.
  3. Dress stall staff in one colour and promoters in another. Visitors find the right person faster, and the stall looks well organised.
  4. Order a couple of extra pieces in your most common sizes for the colleague who joins the team two days before the show.

Crew t-shirts and hoodies for Film City shoots

There is something special about a crew in matching show merch. Film City and the studios around it keep shoots running through the year, and crews love wearing something that says they were part of it. Long after the wrap party, people hold on to these tees and hoodies.

The classic layout is the show name on the front and the department on the back: CAMERA, LIGHTS, PRODUCTION, ART, COSTUME, DIRECTION. Everyone shares the same front, and only the back print changes. For crew tees, DTF printing is often the practical pick, because it needs no setup, prints in full colour and lets each department have its own back print without extra cost per design. If one department is large, say 50 people or more, screen printing that batch brings the per piece price down nicely.

Night shoots, early call times and chilly air-conditioned floors mean hoodies get worn as much as tees. Crews working outdoors near Aarey Colony in the cooler months often ask for custom hoodies with the same front and back layout as their tees. Pullover and zipper hoodies both start at ₹350 before printing.

Send us the crew list as a simple sheet with name, department and size. We pack and label by size, which makes handing everything out on set wonderfully quick.

Custom caps and tote bags for giveaways people actually keep

At a busy exhibition, most flyers end up in a bin near the exit. A canvas tote has a much happier life. People carry it to the market, to college and to work, and your logo travels with them for months. A good cap gets worn on the next outdoor shoot or weekend trip.

A few tips for caps and totes:

  • On totes, a single colour logo on natural canvas looks clean and reads well from across a hall. Screen printing suits this beautifully on runs of 50 or more.
  • On caps, an embroidered front logo looks more finished than a print and stands up to sun and sweat. Our custom caps page shows the styles.
  • Keep the logo large enough to read on a tote, and keep very fine text off caps, since it cannot be embroidered.
  • For welcome kits, polyester backpacks are a smart alternative to canvas totes. You will find both on our custom tote bags page.

Caps start at ₹80 and totes or backpacks at ₹300, each with a minimum of 5.

Print methods for customised t-shirt printing in Goregaon

Every print method is priced per print position, per piece. A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing, from ₹15

Screen printing is the most economical per piece on runs of about 50 or more. It suits designs with 1 to 3 flat colours and looks its best on cotton and blends. Promoter tees for an exhibition and event tees for an office day are classic screen print jobs.

DTF printing, from ₹35

DTF handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup at all. It is ideal for small quantities and individual names, which is exactly why crews love it for department backs. It works on most fabrics. If you are torn between the two, our guide on DTF vs screen printing lays out the differences simply.

Embroidery, from ₹45

Embroidery gives a stitched, premium finish on polos, shirts, caps and jackets. It is the favourite for stall staff polos and office uniforms. Very fine text and photographs cannot be embroidered, so a detailed logo may need a little simplifying, and we show you how it will look on the mockup.

Sublimation, from ₹25

Sublimation dyes the design right into polyester fabric, so it suits white or light polyester garments, all-over designs, jerseys and sports kits. It does not work on cotton.

Choosing fabric and getting sizes right

Fabric decides how a garment feels at the end of a long day. Cotton is soft, breathable and the best base for screen printing. Polyester dries fast and is needed for sublimation. Polycot blends the two and holds its shape well for uniforms worn every day. Sinker is a soft knit used for tees and hoodies, matty is a textured fabric often chosen for polos, and bio-wash cotton has a smoother, softer finish.

For long hours on an exhibition floor under stall lights, many teams choose polyester or polycot polos. For crew tees on outdoor shoots, cotton or bio-wash feels more comfortable. If you want a particular fabric weight, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability.

Frequently asked questions

How much does t-shirt printing cost in Goregaon?

Round neck t-shirts start at ₹120 per piece, and printing is added per position, from ₹15 for screen printing or ₹35 for DTF. For example, 50 tees with a screen printed front and back start at ₹7,500. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp after we see your design.

Can you deliver exhibition t-shirts before my NESCO event?

Share your event dates, hall and stall number when you first message us. We give you a dispatch date with the quote, and courier delivery within Mumbai usually takes 2 to 3 days after that. If the timing looks tight, we tell you before you confirm.

What is the minimum order for custom t-shirts in Goregaon?

The minimum is 5 pieces for round neck tees and polos. Full colour custom printed tees with one design can be ordered from just 2 pieces. Corporate shirts and event costumes have a minimum of 10.

Can each crew member's t-shirt have a different department on the back?

Yes. DTF printing needs no setup, so the back can change by department, such as CAMERA, LIGHTS or PRODUCTION, while the front carries the show name. Send a sheet with each person's name, department and size.

Which print method is best for caps and tote bags?

Embroidery usually suits caps, since it looks finished and holds up outdoors. For canvas totes, screen printing a one or two colour logo is the most economical on runs of about 50 or more. DTF works well for full colour designs on smaller quantities.

Will I see the design before it is printed?

Yes. We share a mockup of your design on the actual garment, and production starts only after you approve it. This is the best moment to check logo size, placement and colours.

Do you deliver to Oshiwara, Malad and Goregaon West?

Yes, we courier to all of these areas and the rest of Mumbai, usually 2 to 3 days after dispatch. Every order is packed and labelled by size, so distribution is easy.
