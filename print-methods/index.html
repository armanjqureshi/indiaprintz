Print methods

Screen printing, DTF, embroidery and sublimation

All four run at our own unit. Pick by your quantity, your number of colours and your fabric. Prices are per print position, per piece, on top of the garment.

Screen printing

From ₹15 per print

Cheapest per piece on big runs with one to three flat colours.

DTF printing

From ₹35 per print

Full colour, photos and gradients, on almost any fabric, from small quantities.

Embroidery

From ₹45 per print

Logos stitched in thread. The premium finish for polos, caps and shirts.

Sublimation

From ₹25 per print

All-over designs dyed into polyester. Made for jerseys and team kits.
