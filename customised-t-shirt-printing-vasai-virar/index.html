Parish feast t-shirts and festival orders in Vasai and Virar

Vasai has a long calendar of parish feasts, village festivals and church events, and most of them need matching t-shirts for volunteers, choirs, youth groups and the people running the stalls. A typical parish feast t-shirt has the parish name or the saint's image on the front and the feast date or the committee name on the back. Christmas carol groups and Easter events bring a second wave of similar orders.

Ganeshotsav is the other big season. Mandals in Virar, Nalasopara and Naigaon usually want the same design for every member, often with the mandal name in large letters on the back. Navratri, Dahi Handi and housing society events add more group orders through the year.

For these orders, a few things help:

  • Book early. Festival weeks are busy, and a dispatch date is only fixed once the design is approved.
  • Send a size count with the order, not just a total. We label and pack by size, so a list like "S 10, M 25, L 30, XL 15" makes distribution at the church or pandal much easier.
  • Keep the colours to 1 to 3 if you are printing 50 or more pieces. Screen printing is the cheapest option at that volume.
  • If the design has a photo of a saint, a church facade or a Ganpati idol with shading, DTF printing will reproduce it in full colour.

Landmarks like Vasai Fort and the coastline also turn up on tees for walks, beach clean-ups and heritage events. We print what you send, so any local artwork works as long as the file is clear.

Football jerseys in Vasai and cricket club kits

Vasai has a strong local football scene, with village teams, parish teams and inter-society tournaments running through the season. Cricket is just as big across Virar, Nalasopara and Naigaon, with box cricket and tennis-ball tournaments almost every weekend in the cooler months. Both need kits that look matched and survive repeated washing.

Football jerseys

We make football and club jerseys from ₹200 per piece with a minimum of 5. For football, polyester is the usual choice because it dries fast and takes sublimation well. With sublimation the whole design, including stripes, sponsor logos, names and numbers, is dyed into the fabric, so nothing cracks or peels off after a muddy match.

Matching shorts

Most clubs want shorts in the same colours. Our custom shorts start at ₹180 per piece, minimum 5, and can carry the club crest and player number on the leg. Ordering jerseys and shorts together keeps the colours consistent across the kit.

Cricket kits

Cricket teams often prefer a polo collar or a round neck jersey with the team name on the front and the player name and number on the back. Individual names on every piece are easiest with DTF or sublimation, because there is no setup cost for each name. If the team wants a cotton feel for practice tees, screen printing or DTF on cotton works well.

School uniforms and house t-shirts for Vasai and Virar

Schools in this belt order regular uniforms, sports day t-shirts, house colour tees and annual day costumes. Our school uniforms start at ₹300 per set with a minimum of 5, in cotton or polycot. The school logo is usually embroidered on the chest pocket, which costs from ₹45 per position and lasts for the life of the garment.

House t-shirts are a different job. Each house needs its own colour, and the print is usually a simple house name and school logo, so screen printing on round neck tees from ₹120 is the economical choice. For NSS units in junior colleges, we make NSS polo t-shirts from ₹180, minimum 5, in cotton, polycot or dry-fit.

Send us the class-wise or house-wise size list along with the design. Packing by size makes distribution in the school much simpler.

Safety jackets for warehouses and units in Vasai East

Vasai East has a large spread of small manufacturers, godowns and warehouses, especially around the industrial areas of Waliv and Sativali. These units order two things most often: hi-vis safety jackets and staff t-shirts.

Our hi-vis safety jackets start at ₹120 per piece, minimum 5, in polyester or polycot. The company name or logo goes on the back so it is visible from a distance, and some units add the department or "Supervisor" on the front. Screen printing or DTF both work on these jackets.

For staff t-shirts, a round neck or polo in polycot holds up to daily wear and repeated washing. An embroidered logo on a polo looks neat for supervisors and office staff, while a screen printed round neck keeps the cost down for larger floor teams. We also make windcheaters for monsoon from ₹300 and track jackets from ₹410 for units that want a warmer layer.

Choosing the fabric

Cotton is soft and breathable, and suits parish tees, school wear and casual events. Polyester is light, quick drying and the only fabric that takes sublimation, which makes it the default for football jerseys. Polycot is a cotton and polyester blend that shrinks less and handles frequent washing, so it works for uniforms and warehouse staff tees. Sinker is a soft knit used for comfortable everyday tees, and bio-wash cotton has a smoother finish with less fuzz.

If you need a particular fabric weight, tell us the GSM you want and we will confirm availability. Our size chart and fabric guide has more detail.

Print methods for customised t-shirt printing in Vasai

The print method is charged per print position, per piece. A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. Front plus back counts as 2 positions.

Screen printing, from ₹15

Screen printing is the cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 or more, with 1 to 3 flat colours. It is best on cotton and blends. Most parish feast tees, mandal tees and house t-shirts are screen printed.

DTF printing, from ₹35

DTF handles full colour, photos and gradients with no setup, so it suits small quantities and individual names on each piece. It works on most fabrics. For a side-by-side look at the two, see DTF and screen printing compared.

Embroidery, from ₹45

Embroidery stitches the logo into the fabric. It looks premium on polos, shirts, caps, jackets and school uniforms. Very fine text and photographs cannot be embroidered, so we may simplify a logo slightly and show you in the mockup.

Sublimation, from ₹25

Sublimation printing dyes the design into polyester, and only works on white or light polyester fabric. It is used for all-over designs, football jerseys and full kits. It does not work on cotton.

Checklist before you order

Having these ready makes the quote faster and avoids changes later:

  1. Your design file, ideally a PDF, AI, PNG or a high resolution image. A clear phone photo of a logo works for a first quote.
  2. The garment type and fabric, for example polyester jerseys or cotton round neck tees.
  3. Colours of the garment and the print.
  4. Print positions, such as front, back or sleeve, and the size of each print.
  5. Size-wise quantities, including any sizes beyond XXL.
  6. Names and numbers list for jerseys, in a single spreadsheet or message.
  7. The date you need the order by, and the delivery address or a request to collect from Dahanu.
