Navratri and garba group t-shirts in Ghatkopar

Ghatkopar is famous for its Navratri garba, and the energy in the lanes during those nine nights is hard to beat. Dance groups, building committees and volunteer teams all want to look like one family on the ground and in the photos. A matching tee does that instantly, and it becomes a keepsake people wear long after the festival ends.

What garba groups usually order

Most groups pick a bright round neck tee with the group name on the front and each dancer's name or number on the back. Volunteer teams often go for a single strong colour with "Volunteer" or "Staff" printed large on the back, so they are easy to spot at the entry gate, in the parking area or near the stage. Some groups add matching caps for the organising team, which looks lovely in the group photo.

For a design with a photo, gradient or lots of colours, DTF printing gives you rich detail with no setup cost. For a bold all-over pattern on polyester, sublimation gives the brightest result and feels like part of the fabric. Individual names on the back are easiest with DTF, since every name can be different without adding a setup charge.

Timing advice for Navratri t-shirts

Send your design and final size list at least two weeks before the day you need the tees. That leaves comfortable room for the quote, mockup approval, last-minute size changes from members, production and courier delivery, which usually takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch. We share a dispatch date with the quote, so you know where you stand before you confirm.

Here is a small secret from years of festival orders: the size list causes more delays than the printing ever does. Use one shared list, collect every member's size before you message us, and you will be dancing in your new tees without any stress.

Choosing a print method that suits your design

A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, the full back or a sleeve. A tee printed on the front and back has 2 print positions, and each one is charged separately. Here is how the four methods compare.

  • Screen printing, from ₹15 per position. The most affordable per piece on runs of about 50 or more. Best for 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends, which makes it ideal for volunteer tees and house colour tees.
  • DTF printing, from ₹35 per position. Full colour, photos and gradients with no setup. Lovely for small quantities and individual names on the back, and it works on most fabrics.
  • Embroidery, from ₹45 per position. Stitched logos with a premium feel for polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered, so keep the logo clean and simple.
  • Sublimation, from ₹25 per position. Dyes the design into polyester, on white or light fabric. Ideal for all-over garba designs and sports jerseys, though it does not work on cotton.

If you are torn between the two most popular options, our guide on DTF vs screen printing breaks it down. The short version is that screen printing shines on large runs with simple colours, and DTF shines on small runs or detailed artwork. Not sure at all? Fabrics that handle garba nights and Mumbai humidity

Garba nights are long, crowded and warm, and Mumbai stays humid for most of the year. The right fabric makes the difference between a tee your group loves and one that sits in a cupboard.

Polyester and dry-fit for dancing and sports

Polyester, which most people call dry-fit, is light and dries quickly. It clings less when you sweat, so it is the happier choice for dancers and players. It also takes sublimation, which means you can have vivid all-over designs that will not crack. Our sublimation printing page explains how it works on jerseys and festival tees.

Cotton and blends for offices and daily wear

Cotton is soft and comfortable through a full office day, and it takes screen printing and DTF very well. It holds sweat longer than polyester, so it is less suited to hours of dancing. Polycot, a cotton and polyester blend, sits nicely in between and keeps its shape after many washes, which is why so many staff uniform orders use it. If you have a GSM in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. Our size chart and fabric guide puts measurements and fabric notes in one place.
