Navratri and garba group t-shirts in Ghatkopar

Ghatkopar is known for its big Navratri garba events. Dance groups, building committees and volunteer teams order matching tees so they look like one group on the ground and in photos. Volunteers handling entry, parking and stage duty also need tees that make them easy to spot in a crowd.

What garba groups usually order

Most groups pick a bright round neck tee with the group name on the front and names or numbers on the back. Volunteer teams often prefer a single colour with "Volunteer" or "Staff" in large print on the back so it can be read from a distance. Some groups add caps for the organising team.

For a full colour design with a photo or gradient, DTF printing works well. For an all-over pattern on polyester, sublimation gives the brightest result. Names on the back for each dancer are easiest with DTF because there is no setup cost per name.

Send your design and the final size list at least two weeks before the first day you need the tees. That gives time for the quote, the mockup approval, any size changes from group members, production and courier delivery, which takes 2 to 3 days after dispatch. We give a dispatch date with the quote, so you know exactly where you stand before you pay.

The most common delay is the size list, not the printing. Collect sizes from every member before you send the order, and use one shared list so nobody is counted twice.

Print methods and which one to choose

A print position is one print area, such as the front chest, full back or a sleeve. A tee printed on the front and back has 2 print positions, and each position is charged separately.

  • Screen printing, from ₹15 per position. Cheapest per piece on runs of about 50 or more. Best for 1 to 3 flat colours on cotton and blends. Good for volunteer tees and house colour tees.
  • DTF printing, from ₹35 per position. Full colour, photos and gradients with no setup. Good for small quantities and individual names on the back. Works on most fabrics.
  • Embroidery, from ₹45 per position. Stitched logos for polos, shirts, caps and jackets. Very fine text and photos cannot be embroidered.
  • Sublimation, from ₹25 per position. Dyes the design into polyester only, on white or light fabric. Ideal for all-over garba designs and sports jerseys. It does not work on cotton.

If you are unsure between the two most common options, read our guide on DTF vs screen printing. The short version is that screen printing wins on large runs with simple colours, and DTF wins on small runs or detailed artwork.

Fabrics for dancing, heat and daily wear

Garba nights are long, crowded and warm, and Mumbai is humid for most of the year. Fabric choice matters as much as the print.

Polyester and dry-fit for garba and sports

Polyester, the fabric most people call dry-fit, is light and dries quickly. It does not cling as much when you sweat, which makes it the better pick for dancers and players. It also takes sublimation, so you can print bright all-over designs that will not crack. Our sublimation printing page explains how it works on jerseys and festival tees.

Cotton and blends for offices and daily wear

Cotton is soft and comfortable for a full office day, and it takes screen printing and DTF well. It holds sweat longer than polyester, so it is less suited to hours of dancing. Polycot, a cotton and polyester blend, sits in between and keeps its shape after many washes, which is why many staff uniform orders use it. If you have a GSM in mind, tell us the GSM you want and we confirm availability. The size chart and fabric guide lists measurements and fabric notes in one place.

Order checklist before you send your design

Going through this list before you message us saves a round of back and forth.

  • Colours. Tee colour and print colours. Share your brand colour code if you have one.
  • Print positions. Front, back, sleeve, and what goes on each.
