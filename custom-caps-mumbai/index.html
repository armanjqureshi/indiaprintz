Questions

Custom caps: common questions

Is embroidery or print better on caps?

Embroidery for most logos. It holds its shape on the curved front and does not peel. DTF works for very detailed or photographic logos.

What is the minimum order for custom caps?

5 pieces.

Can you embroider on the side or back?

Yes. Each extra area is one more position.

Are caps adjustable?

Our caps have an adjustable back so one size fits most adults. Ask us if you need kids' sizes.
